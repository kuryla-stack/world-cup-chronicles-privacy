# World Cup Chronicles Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 September 2026

World Cup Chronicles is an offline reference application. This policy explains the small amount of information the application keeps locally so that its settings work.

## Information stored on the device

The application stores whether onboarding is complete, favourite tournament IDs, favourite player IDs, favourite moment IDs, the selected theme, motion preference and text-size preference. These values are stored in the application’s private preferences area on the device.

## Information not collected

The application does not request an account, name, contact detail, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or payment information. It does not send the local preference values to a server. It does not use advertising identifiers or third-party measurement services.

## Network and sharing

The archive is designed to work without a network connection. The application does not provide a social sharing or upload feature. No personal information is sold, rented or disclosed.

## Your choices

You can remove saved items and preferences from the Settings screen using Reset local data. You can also remove all application data through the Android system settings.

## Children

The application is a general sports reference. It does not knowingly request personal information from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the owner will publish an updated version with a new effective date.
